Where do the rates come from?

Fiat rates come from ExchangeRate-API. Cryptocurrency rates are Powered by CoinGecko. Rates are for information only and shouldn’t be used for financial decisions.

How often do rates update?

Automatically once a day when you open the app. Pull down on the list, or tap “Rates Updated” in Settings, to refresh right away.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The last downloaded rates are saved on your device and used until the next successful refresh.

Why do some amounts have more decimals than others?

Reito shows each amount with as many digits as it needs to stay accurate, so a tiny crypto amount isn’t rounded down to zero while dollars stay as dollars.

How do I add or remove a currency?

Tap + on the main screen and choose Currencies or Tokens. To reorder or remove, tap ··· and choose Reorder.

What do tips unlock?

Nothing — every feature is free. Tips in the About screen are an optional way to support development. They’re processed by Apple, and there’s nothing to restore.
