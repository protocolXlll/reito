Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

In short: Reito does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you set up in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Reito saves your chosen currencies, their order, the amount you last entered, your settings (haptic feedback, flags, appearance) and the most recently downloaded exchange rates. This data is kept only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Exchange rate requests

To show current rates, the app downloads them from two public services:

  • ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) for fiat currencies
  • CoinGecko for cryptocurrencies

These requests contain no personal information, no identifiers and nothing you typed into the app. They only ask for the latest rates. As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. Their handling of it is governed by their own privacy policies.

Tips

Reito offers optional tips through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment. The developer never receives your name, payment details or Apple ID, only an anonymous confirmation that the purchase went through.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Reito contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Reito collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy
